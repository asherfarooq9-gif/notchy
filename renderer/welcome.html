Welcome to Notchy

Notchy lives in a small notch at the top of your screen. Before it starts, pick a look and choose what it is allowed to do. You can change every choice later in Settings.

Pick your look

  • Computer control (typing, Wi-Fi, shut down) stays off until you turn it on in Settings.
  • Notchy shows WhatsApp message pop-ups in the notch. You can change which apps in Settings.
  • Open the panel with Control+Alt+N. Click the Notchy icon near the clock for Settings.
